What to look at, concretely

  • Toggle Dark ⇄ Light on any page (bottom-right control) — confirm the port reads correctly in both.
  • Pick aurora A or B (above), or a third option if neither feels right — this is the one required decision (O1).
  • Flip Motion → Reduced on any page with an aurora button — confirm the static-halo fallback is acceptable (this also honors your real OS reduced-motion setting automatically).
  • On the editor mockup, switch "Dock anatomy" → On to see labeled callouts for split / tab strip / floating group.
  • Skim TOKENS.md for anything marked PROPOSAL — those are values doc 06 named but didn't fix (viewport palette hexes, spacing/density scale, icon set) and are open to a quick amend, not just the aurora call.
