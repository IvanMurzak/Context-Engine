Signature flourish — pick one

The owner reviewed the first pass and rejected both aurora variants — the rotating monochrome ink-alpha conic halo on the Play button "looks bad." This page presents six genuinely distinct, fully-executed replacements (plus the retired original for direct comparison), each shown live on a realistic Play button inside a real play-bar strip. Nothing here is a screenshot — flip Theme and Motion below and every treatment updates for real.

✅ Owner picked — #6 Pulse of Work (bloom −50%, activity-linked speeds + Error state; 2026-07-19)

The brief: doc 06 §2 names the signature flourish as one deliberate moment of "glow" on the Play / active-action button, used sparingly. The rotating conic halo satisfied the letter of that spec but not the spirit — it read as an aimless, muddy smear rather than a considered flourish. Every option below fixes that a different way: some stay strictly monochrome, one is the sanctioned rare break into color, one ties the glow to the button's actual state instead of decorating it unconditionally.

Compare them here, then try your favorite live in the full editor chrome via the new Flourish control in editor.html's Mockup Controls panel (bottom-right) — same six options, same selector, wired to the real Play button.

✅ Owner picked Pulse of Work — the worker's original straight recommendation (below) had been Ink Bloom

Ink Bloom. It fixes the actual complaint — nothing rotates, nothing smears — while staying the most literal read of "monochrome-glow-ui": a controlled, breathing bloom plus a crisp static ring reads as considered and premium rather than decorative. It's also the lowest- risk pick for a second review after a rejection: pure monochrome, no new tokens, no new interaction model.

Strongest alternates, in order: Passing Light if you want something that feels more like a "premium product" moment (closest to an Apple/Stripe button sheen) and are fine with it being a distinct periodic event rather than an ambient glow; Pulse of Work if you'd rather the flourish carry real information (it's the most "systems-considered" answer, reusing hues already reserved for exactly this — its only downside is a less distinctive resting/idle state before Play is pressed). Living Line is the safest possible fallback if even Ink Bloom feels like too much — it's almost entirely invisible until you look for it, which may or may not be enough presence for "the one signature moment" doc 06 asks for.
